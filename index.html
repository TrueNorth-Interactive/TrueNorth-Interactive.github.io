Cards you earn in our other games are always free to add, even after the trial ends.

MorningMission

An alarm that only turns off when you finish a mission. No snooze trap — dance, push-ups, maths, a photo match, or a wolf howl, and you are actually out of bed.

  • ◆40 missions — move, brain, camera, voice and tap missions, each under 60 seconds.
  • ◆Surprise me — let the app pick a mission at random each morning.
  • ◆Never trapped — a kind timeout always lets you out, with no scolding.
  • ◆Every mission earns a collector card — complete a mission, collect its card.

Point Fingers

The who's-most-likely-to party game. A question drops, everyone points at who fits best — the most-pointed-at player takes the roast, and the night ends with an Awards Show. Pass the phone, no install needed.

Eight editions — Kids, Family, Sports, Teens, Friends, Work, Adults, Spicy. The host picks one sealed edition per room, and it stays sealed all night.

  • ◆Know Me, Twists and Stakes — secret answers, dare cards and a twist keep every round moving.
  • ◆An Awards Show to close the night — Most Pointed At, Under the Radar, and more, fit to the edition.
  • ◆Guests join free — the host's link opens in any phone browser. No install, no account.
  • ◆Earnable ScanVault cards — play well and take home real collector cards for your ScanVault binder.

Play now — free on the web

The studio

Small studio,
six ways to play.

True North Interactive is an independent app studio in Ajax, Ontario. We don't make one kind of app — we make the ones we wanted to exist: a scanner that treats your cards like they matter, a tower-defense saga where every victory earns a card, an alarm that actually gets you out of bed, a zombie city where dying doesn't end the game — it changes sides — a party game where the whole room points at who fits, and a daily history Short that tells you what happened on this date.

ScanVault — collect and value your cards. Hollowreign — collect guardians and hold the breach. MorningMission — earn a card by finishing a morning mission. OUTBREAK — survive the city, extract, or join the infected. Point Fingers — point at who fits, take the roast. On This Day — a new history story every day.

The same creed runs through all six: try it free first, one-time pricing instead of subscriptions, no dark patterns. Every feature ships only if we'd want it ourselves.
